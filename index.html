1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number with country code, and password. Your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form; we cannot read it.
  • Profile photo: if you choose to add one from your camera or gallery.
  • Bookings: the salon, services, specialist, date and time you choose, coupon codes used, notes you add, and the booking status (booked, waiting, in progress, completed or cancelled).
  • Reviews and ratings: the stars, comments and tags you leave after a visit. Your name and profile photo are shown next to your review.
  • Favourites and preferences: salons you save, your app language and notification setting.
  • Messages to us: anything you send to our support team, including reports of harassment.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: see section 3.
  • Technical data: app version, device type, operating system, language, IP address, and server logs (date, time and the request made). These help us keep the Service secure and fix problems.
  • Saved on your device: login tokens (in your phone's secure storage), recent searches, your chosen location and map radius. These stay on your phone.

Payments: StyleHub does not currently take online payments. You pay the salon directly. We do not collect or store card or bank details.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, and to verify your email with a one-time code.
  • To show salons, services, prices, offers and free time slots near you.
  • To make, change, cancel and track your bookings, and to share the booking details with the salon you booked.
  • To show your reviews to other users and calculate salon ratings.
  • To send you service emails (verification codes, password reset), in your chosen language.
  • To keep the Service safe: preventing fraud, spam, misuse and harassment, and enforcing our rules.
  • To comply with the law and respond to lawful requests from authorities.
  • To understand and improve the Service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Location data

  • With your permission, StyleHub uses your phone's location (GPS) to show salons near you, distances, and your position on the map. You can instead choose a point on the map yourself in Profile → Location.
  • Your location is used only while you use the app. We do not track you in the background.
  • To show your area name (for example "F-7, Islamabad") we send your approximate coordinates to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service.
  • You can turn location off at any time in your phone settings. The app keeps working, but nearby results and distances will be less accurate.

5. Harassment, abuse and legal action

StyleHub has zero tolerance for harassment of any kind. This protects every person who uses or works with the Service: customers, salon owners, hairdressers, specialists and our own team.

What counts as harassment

Harassment includes, but is not limited to, any of the following, whether done through the app, in reviews, by phone, by message, or in person in connection with a booking:

  • Sexual harassment: unwanted sexual comments, advances, requests, images or contact.
  • Threats, intimidation, blackmail or violence of any kind.
  • Abusive, insulting, hateful or degrading language, including in reviews.
  • Discrimination or hate based on gender, religion, ethnicity, caste, nationality, disability, age or any other characteristic.
  • Stalking: repeatedly contacting, following or monitoring a person who does not want it, including misuse of a phone number or address obtained through a booking.
  • Sharing someone's photos, phone number, address or other private information without their consent.
  • Fake, defamatory or malicious reviews, false bookings made to disturb a salon, or impersonating another person.
  • Any behaviour that makes another person feel unsafe.

What we do

  • Remove content such as abusive reviews or profile photos, without notice.
  • Suspend or permanently ban the account responsible, and block new accounts made by the same person.
  • Preserve evidence: we may keep the relevant account details, bookings, reviews, messages and logs for as long as needed for an investigation or legal case, even if the account is deleted.
  • Report to authorities: we may report the matter to the police, the FIA Cybercrime Wing, or other competent authorities, and share the evidence with them.
  • Take legal action: StyleHub reserves the right to take, or support the victim in taking, civil and criminal legal action against the individual responsible under applicable laws, including the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 (offences against the dignity and modesty of a person, cyber stalking), the Pakistan Penal Code, the Protection against Harassment of Women at the Workplace Act, 2010 where it applies, and any other relevant law. This includes claims for damages and costs.

How to report harassment

If you are harassed by anyone through StyleHub, email support@your-domain.com with the subject "Harassment report". Include the booking reference (shown on your receipt) if there is one, the date, what happened, and any screenshots. We treat every report seriously and confidentially, and we do not tell the reported person who made the report unless the law requires it.

If you are in immediate danger, call the police first (15 in Pakistan).

6. How long we keep information

  • Account and profile: while your account is active.
  • Bookings and receipts: while your account is active and as long as needed for accounting, disputes and legal obligations.
  • Verification codes: a few minutes; login sessions until they expire or you log out.
  • Server logs: a limited period, for security and troubleshooting.
  • Harassment and fraud cases: as long as needed for the investigation or legal proceedings (see section 5).

7. Security

We protect your information with reasonable technical and organisational measures: data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, login tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage, and access to our database is restricted. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and do not share it. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will notify you as required by law.

8. Your rights and choices

  • See and correct: view and edit your name, phone number and photo in Profile → Account.
  • Language and notifications: change them in Profile.
  • Location: switch between GPS and a point you choose, or turn location off in your phone settings.
  • Copy of your data or deletion: contact us (see section 12). We reply within 30 days.

9. Deleting your account

To delete your account and personal data, email support@your-domain.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete or anonymise your account, profile photo, favourites and reviews within 30 days. We may keep booking records needed for legal or accounting reasons, and information needed for an open harassment, fraud or legal case, only for as long as required.

10. Children

StyleHub is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Users under 18 should use StyleHub with the permission of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will change the "Effective date" above and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
